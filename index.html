In parallelogram $ABCD$, base $AB = 9\text{ cm}$ and perpendicular height from $D$ to $AB$ is $7\text{ cm}$. Calculate the area of parallelogram $ABCD$.
› Show solution
$\text{Area} = \text{base} \times \text{height} = 9 \times 7 = 63\text{ cm}^2$
02
Find the roots of the quadratic equation $2x^2 - 5x + 3 = 0$ using the quadratic formula.
› Show solution
Discriminant $D = b^2 - 4ac = (-5)^2 - 4(2)(3) = 25 - 24 = 1$
$x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{D}}{2a} = \frac{5 \pm 1}{4}$
$x_1 = \frac{6}{4} = \frac{3}{2}$, and $x_2 = \frac{4}{4} = 1$
03
A tower stands vertically on the ground. From a point on the ground $30\text{ m}$ away from the foot of the tower, the angle of elevation of its top is $30^\circ$. Find the height of the tower.
› Show solution
$\tan(30^\circ) = \frac{\text{Height}}{30}$
$\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{h}{30} \implies h = \frac{30}{\sqrt{3}} = 10\sqrt{3}\text{ m} \approx 17.32\text{ m}$

Geometry & Mensuration Cheatsheet

Standard CBSE / NCERT exam reference formulas

🔷 2D Shapes (Area & Perimeter)

Parallelogram

$$\text{Area} = b \times h$$

$$\text{Perimeter} = 2(a + b)$$

Triangle

$$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2}bh$$

$$\text{Heron's: } \sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}$$

Trapezium

$$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2}(a + b)h$$

Circle & Sector

$$\text{Area} = \pi r^2 \quad | \quad C = 2\pi r$$

$$\text{Sector} = \frac{\theta}{360^\circ} \times \pi r^2$$

📦 3D Solids (Surface Area & Volume)

Cylinder

$$\text{TSA} = 2\pi r(r + h)$$

$$\text{Volume} = \pi r^2 h$$

Cone

$$\text{CSA} = \pi rl \quad (l = \sqrt{r^2 + h^2})$$

$$\text{Volume} = \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h$$

Sphere & Hemisphere

$$\text{Sphere } V = \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3 \quad (\text{TSA} = 4\pi r^2)$$

$$\text{Hemi } V = \frac{2}{3}\pi r^3 \quad (\text{TSA} = 3\pi r^2)$$

Cuboid

$$\text{TSA} = 2(lb + bh + hl)$$

$$\text{Volume} = l \times b \times h$$

📐 Coordinate Geometry & Trigonometry

Distance & Section Formula

$$d = \sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2 + (y_2-y_1)^2}$$

$$\left(\frac{m_1 x_2 + m_2 x_1}{m_1 + m_2}, \frac{m_1 y_2 + m_2 y_1}{m_1 + m_2}\right)$$

Trig Identities

$$\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta = 1$$

$$\tan\theta = \frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta} = \frac{\text{Opp}}{\text{Adj}}$$
